About Us

Tulsi Madras is a celebration of Indian textile heritage and timeless elegance. Founded in Chennai and once known as Rasvriti, we believe that a saree is more than fabric—it is a living entity woven with life and love. Each garment carries stories of artisans and tradition, thoughtfully curated to connect you with the rich cultural tapestry of India.Our collection spans exquisite Kanchipuram silks, vibrant Banarasi zari work, earthy Tussar, and delicate Organza, alongside breathable, stylish cotton sarees and contemporary salwar suits. Whether it’s a grand wedding, a festive celebration, or everyday grace, our range blends tradition and modern design in perfect harmony.At Tulsi Madras, quality and craftsmanship are non-negotiable. We partner with skilled weavers to ensure every weave, embellishment, and finish meets the highest standards. Our philosophy, “Elegance You Can Wear,” reflects our commitment to creating garments that feel as beautiful as they look.
